# Fixed-pack model for supervisory review

The 5.5 kg RF-MPC plant supplies its actual body pose, foot positions, geometry and solved ground forces. The motor/transmission are a separate Mini Cheetah design choice. The battery is the manuscript's experimentally characterized cell, replicated in a **single fixed 4S6P pack across all episodes** (2 Ah/cell, 12 Ah pack). The 5.5 kg is a stipulated total simulated mass, not a weighed assembly. No new mass, inertia, contact constraints or QP weights were fitted to get a better result. The 12 V legacy sizing target does not set terminal voltage. Existing saved runs without ENERGY still reconstruct the historical proxy.

## What changes and why

| Quantity | Historical evidence | Review profile |
|---|---|---|
| Knee torque | Digitized single-joint waveform, CSV columns 1–3 = seconds, phase, signed joint Nm | Preserve the CSV. Stance uses actual force and RF-MPC leg geometry; swing adds the single-leg signed knee template for that leg's FSM time, with the existing inverse-square duration scaling. Do not add the stance template to contact torque. Direct transfer of swing amplitude/sign is a working assumption. |
| Hip pitch | Project log describes 6.114 Nm as a **per-hip 95th percentile** for aggressive motions; historical config labels 6.114 as the four-hip total | The symmetric aggregate magnitude of that bound would be 24.456 Nm, not a constant measurement throughout a trot. Calculate each hip's contact load instead. Keep the old absolute-current reconstruction for historical comparisons only. |
| Transmission | Hip 6; historical knee 6/1.55 | Use N = motor speed / joint speed: 6 abduction, 6 hip pitch, 9.33 knee. At 1 Nm, eta=.9, motor torque is .1191 Nm instead of .2870 Nm at the knee. Holding all else fixed, this changes motor current by a factor of .415 and increases motor speed by the reciprocal. |
| Motor constants | The project extracted .0909 Nm/A from a datasheet | Preserve it with the historical model. MIT's model uses flux linkage times pole pairs .05, effective torque per peak phase amp .075, and its bus-voltage envelope uses .10 V/(rad/s). These numbers have different definitions. We do **not** claim the original extraction is wrong. A current-loop integral gain Ki does not convert watts to battery amps. No simulated current PI loop. |
| Electrical power | Magnitudes of knee plus hip winding current previously fed a battery-current input | Compute power at each motor, sum DC load, solve the pack, and book exactly one current/charge/energy increment for each successful integrated interval, including startup. An actuator-limit terminal includes the interval that diagnosed the exceedance; its final position is a requested trajectory, not evidence of achievable motion. |

## Equations and boundaries

1. Use the repository's inverse kinematics before/after the successful time step for joint angular velocity. The body-frame leg Jacobian maps **world ground reaction on the leg** to support effort as -J' R' F. A finite-displacement virtual-work test checks the sign, leg geometry and axes. The simulated legs are massless: no claim to have identified full limb inverse dynamics. Hip/abduction swing inertia, impacts and load-dependent compliance remain uncalibrated.
2. Add joint friction and transform the requested joint torque to the motor shaft. Divide by gear efficiency for driving and multiply by it for backdriving; motor speed is N times joint speed. Torque at zero speed still dissipates winding heat. The voltage **screen** reuses MIT's approximate line-voltage relation, R I + 2 lambda omega. Enforce the motor torque ceiling and compare required voltage to loaded pack terminal voltage.
3. Under a balanced sinusoidal winding convention with peak phase amps, copper watts = 3/4 times line resistance times peak amps squared. Interpreting MIT's equivalent .173 ohm as line resistance is a modeling assumption; the header does not specify a winding test. This convention also makes the loss coefficient consistent with MIT's torque/back-EMF factors (.075/.10). Compute each motor's shaft watts plus copper watts and floor at zero **per motor**: dissipative braking with no recovered energy or cross-drive exchange. The .90 drivetrain efficiency and friction terms together are a conservative approximation; they could overlap. Baseline inverter efficiency 1 and auxiliary power 0 are explicit exclusions, not measurements. Resulting Wh is modeled drive demand; it does not establish whole-robot runtime.
4. For one interval, eliminate the three polarization branch updates from Vcell = E - R Icell. With a_k=exp(-dt/(R_k C_k)), E=OCV-sum(a_k Vrc_k) and R=R0+sum((1-a_k)R_k). Use the high-voltage discharge root for P/(Ns Np)=Icell (E-R Icell). Negative discriminants are reported as unsupplyable. Integrate pack charge in As and terminal energy in Wh; the EKF consumes cell current and cell voltage, not total pack current. It uses the manuscript noise values **per update at the MPC sampling rate**. A simulated voltage from the same cell model is a software consistency test, not independent estimator validation.
5. The pack state owns these quantities. The captured environment, chunk summaries, target crossing and objectives consume that record; the function-based simulator and MAIN use the same load and pack functions. Historical proxy-only sweep scripts reject the fixed-pack feedback contract rather than emit mislabeled battery measurements. The old centroidal-to-current and design-sizing functions remain available for separately labeled comparisons.

## Reproduce and evaluate

Use MATLAB R2026a with Optimization and Reinforcement Learning toolboxes. Check out this source Git repository alongside the monitor repository named RL-MPC-Monitor. Start in a clean, **fresh checkout** without a saved rlEnv_MPC_R.mat. Run:

    run_fixed_pack_review_suite(fullfile(fileparts(pwd),'BeyondTorque-Local-Artifacts','my-new-review'))

The suite refuses to overwrite evidence. It writes tests.csv, a manifest with the commit and input hashes, a captured two-decision environment/reset test, mission JSONs and speed_probe.csv. Raw captured states stay outside Git. The speed scan deliberately observes exceedances to count them; the mission cases enforce limits on the first affected step. The two modes answer different questions. The short 3 m target is a software acceptance case only.

**RL review order:** establish at least one feasible deadline mission and voltage margin under the intended SOC range; compare fixed and rule-based policies at the same target/deadline; inspect applied R, speed and acceleration versus actual Wh and timing; only then select observations/actions or fit a reward. First review the swing knee speed and motor voltage conventions, including low-SOC sag. Consider exposing voltage headroom and remaining distance/time; consider a bounded swing-time action only after MPC transition tests. Deadline completion and actuator feasibility precede energy preference. Do not use fixed 50% SOC or a change to the speed cap to manufacture a ranking. No SOH or training promotion.
